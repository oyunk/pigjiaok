In just four decades Futian has transformed into a bustling global tech hub, almost unrecognizable from its former self. Piggy was saddened to see the change, but it never forgot its roots🌽. With the spirit of a true Made in China 🇨 🇳 pioneer pig, Piggy is now on a mission to bring its love of sustainable snacking to the buzz of modern city life. Its ultimate goal?
